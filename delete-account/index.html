Delete your account

Request account and associated data deletion for the iOS or Android app.

Email a deletion request to scoreappau@gmail.com

Request deletion without the app

  1. Email scoreappau@gmail.com from the email address associated with your ScoreAU account.
  2. Use the subject ScoreAU account deletion request and state that you want your account and associated data deleted.
  3. We may ask you to verify account ownership before completing deletion. Do not send your password, sign-in tokens or payment details.

You do not need to reinstall ScoreAU. We process verified account-deletion requests within 30 days.

Delete within the app

While signed in, open Settings → Account → Delete Account and confirm. A successful deletion signs you out. If deletion fails, the app shows an error so you can retry or contact us.

What is deleted

Deletion removes your account profile, account sign-in credentials, saved account follows, account sessions and linked notification-device records from our server. It does not delete your Apple or Google account. Deletion cannot be undone.

App preferences and cached scores can remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. Independently retained crash diagnostics and aggregate operational records follow the retention periods in our Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions

Deleting a ScoreAU account does not cancel an Apple App Store subscription. Manage any existing subscription in your Apple account settings. Android does not offer purchases in its first release.
